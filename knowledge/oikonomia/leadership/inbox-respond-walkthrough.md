Four steps. The inbox is ordered for you: decisions first, then work, then things to consider.

## Open the Leadership Inbox

Destination: inbox

Leadership Inbox is under **Oversight** in the sidebar. The counts beside **Approvals**, **Actions** and **Attention** tell you how much is waiting.

## Decide the approvals

Choose **Approvals**. For each ask, choose **Open context** if you need to read the record, then **Approve**, **Decline** with a reason, or **Ask a question**.

## Answer the actions

Choose **Actions**. If you will do it later, choose **Put on my week** so it has a day. If it is already done, choose **Completed**. If you cannot do it, choose **Can't do it** and say why.

## Note what needs your attention

Choose **Attention**. Read each ask and choose **Noted**. Reports under **Flagged by their category** were not addressed to you; open one only if you want to read it.

## Finish what you put on your week

Destination: weekly-agenda

Do the work on your Weekly Agenda. When it is done, come back to the Leadership Inbox and choose **Completed** on the ask — ticking the agenda item alone does not tell the person who asked.
