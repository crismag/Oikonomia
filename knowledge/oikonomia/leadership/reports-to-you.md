Reports to you is under Oversight in the sidebar. A report here is **information**: it is here to read, and asks nothing of you unless its author said so. What was asked of you is in the [Leadership Inbox](destination:inbox).

### Your reports

Your five most recently updated Leadership Reports, with their stage. **All your reports** opens Leadership Reports.

### Shared with you

Leadership Reports other leaders wrote that reached you — because you are named, because you are in a group it went to, or because you are part of its leadership audience. Newest first.

Search by title or period, and filter by **All**, **New** or **Read**. Opening a report marks it read. Read is not acknowledgement: it does not mean you agreed or acted. The newest 25 are shown; the rest are under [Shared With Me](destination:leadership-reports.shared).

### Goals

This year's goals, grouped under **People who report to you**, **Ministries** and **Other groups**. Open a person, ministry or group to see theirs, and a goal to read it. Hidden when there are none.

### Work reports

Listed only when there are any. These are a different kind of record from Leadership Reports; each opens as a [work record](topic:work.record-page).

When some reports are closed to you, the page says how many — never which.
