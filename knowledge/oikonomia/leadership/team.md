Team Overview is under Oversight in the sidebar. It shows how other leaders' work is going. It is not a place to do the work, and it is not a ranking.

### Your scope

The line under the title says what you are looking at and why:

- a member of a leadership group your church named for the **whole church** sees all leaders;
- a member of one named for **your campus** sees that campus's leaders;
- a **ministry lead** sees the leaders of the ministries they lead;
- anyone else sees how the campus is doing — **counted, not named**.

Leaders are people who lead a ministry or a gathering, or who write reports.

### What it shows

- **Leaders**, **On track**, **Reporting**, **Need attention** and **Overdue**. Choose Need attention or Overdue to filter the page.
- **Needs attention** — obligations due soon or overdue, with **Look into it**. See [Follow up from Team Overview](topic:team.follow-up).
- **Reporting** — expected, submitted, late, not submitted, and how many you have not read. **Open Reports** goes to Reports to you.
- **Leaders** (when names are visible) — each leader by work area. Select one for a quick view.
- **Work areas**, **Reporting over time** and, when names are visible, **Reporting consistency**.

Reports counted here are only those you may read. Seeing that something is behind never opens the record behind it, and oversight does not make you a report's reader.
