1. Open [Reports to you](destination:reports-to-you), under **Oversight** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Shared with you**, type in **Search shared reports** — it matches the title or the reporting period.
3. Choose **New** to see only reports you have not opened, or **Read** for those you have. Choose **All** to clear it.
4. Choose a report to open it.

The newest 25 are listed. If there are more, choose **All shared with you** or **See them all in Leadership Reports**.

### New and Read

A report is **New** until you open it from here or from the Leadership Inbox; then it is **Read**. That is all it means. Read is not agreement, approval or a promise to act, and the author is not told you agreed.

A report marked **Confidential** is read by opening it, and its author can see who opened it.

### Not listed

A report appears here only if it reached you — you were named, it went to a group you belong to, or it was shared with leadership you are part of. When some are closed to you, the page gives a count and never names them.
