### 1. Check what you are looking at

The line under **Team Overview** names your scope and why you have it — for example _All leaders · You are part of Church Leadership_. Everything on the page is counted inside that scope only, so a small number is small for that scope, not for the church.

If it says _You can see how the campus is doing, not who is behind_, you are seeing totals without names. Items read **Someone in** your campus.

### 2. Narrow to what is behind

Choose the **Need attention** or **Overdue** card. The page then shows only those. Choose **show everything** to go back.

### 3. Look into it

Under **Needs attention**, each row says why it is flagged (**Overdue**, **Due soon**, **Incomplete**, **Not yet read** and so on), the area and the period. **Look into it** opens the page where that work lives. When names are hidden, it opens [Reports to you](destination:reports-to-you) instead.

With names visible, you can also select a leader under **Leaders** for a quick view of their areas.

### 4. Act where the work is

Team Overview has no controls of its own. To ask a leader for something, open the record and use **Does this need anything from leadership?** — see [Ask leadership for something](topic:asks.raise). Seeing that something is late never opens a record you could not already read.
