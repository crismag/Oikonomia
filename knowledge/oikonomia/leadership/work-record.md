A work record is opened from Reports to you (**Work reports**), a person's page (**Work and reports**) or a Leadership Journal list (**Shared with you**). It is a different record from a Leadership Report.

### Reading it

- The header names the kind of record, its context, its status, and who opened it.
- **Where this stands** — the current state in one line.
- Any decisions, then **Unresolved** questions.
- **Report** or **Record** — its sections. A section held more tightly than the record is shown by title only.
- **Working material** — documents registered against it; **Open** goes to where each one lives. Edit them there.
- Beside it (under **Details, people and activity** on a phone): **Audience**, **Sent to** or **Reviewers**, **Assigned**, **Participants**, **Context** and **Activity**.

If the page shows only routing details (kind, context, owner, last activity), your access is to that summary, not the content.

### What you can do

The **Yours to move** card appears only when you own the record or review it:

- the owner of a draft, or one returned with changes, can **Submit for review**;
- where the record is one a process reviews, its reviewers can **Start review**, **Acknowledge** or **Request changes** (with a note);
- the owner or a reviewer can **Mark resolved**.

If someone asked you for an action on this record, **Asked of you** offers **Put on my week**; answer it in the Leadership Inbox. To ask leadership for something yourself, use **Does this need anything from leadership?**

Readers who are neither owner nor reviewer see no controls: reading a record is not authority over it.
