Nothing is done on Home itself. Every row opens the record the work belongs to; do the work there, and Home catches up.

### The button at the top

- When something needs you, the first button is the next step for the most pressing item — for example **Continue the report**, **Write the report** or **Open the week**. It opens that item.
- **Add to the week** opens [Weekly Agenda](destination:weekly-agenda), where you choose **Add**.

### Needs your attention

Parts of your own leadership cycle that are overdue, due today or tomorrow, or sent back to you. Each row names its area, shows how late or close it is, and its next step. Selecting it opens that area — the gathering, the report, the week.

**My Progress** on the card opens the whole cycle.

### Asked of you

Things another leader has asked of you. The word on the row says what they want: **Decide** (an approval), **Act** (an action) or **Consider** (something for your attention). Selecting a row opens the report or record the ask was made on, where the **Asked of you** card lets you put it on your week. **Leadership Inbox** on the card lists every ask.

### This week

What is still ahead on your week: entries and unfinished tasks from today to the end of the week, up to six. Selecting one opens it on Weekly Agenda. A task from a meeting opens its meeting note when you may read it, otherwise its day on the week.

### What you are carrying

Parts of your cycle already under way, or not yet started. Selecting one opens where it is done.

### LifeGroup, Your reports, Shared with others

- **LifeGroup** — gatherings you lead, from the start of this week, then any that nobody leads yet, marked **Needs a leader**. **Assign to me** on such a row means you could take it; selecting opens the gathering. Gatherings other leaders lead are on the schedule, not here.
- **Your reports** — your open Leadership Reports; selecting opens the report.
- **Shared with others** — ministries you lead or serve in; selecting opens the ministry.

When a card is empty, it says so and offers the place to start, such as **Plan the week** or **Write a report**.
