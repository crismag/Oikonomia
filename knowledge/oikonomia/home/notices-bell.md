The bell sits in the top bar, beside the account menu.

### What it counts

The number counts what is **new for you**:

- something another leader asked of you, and
- a task somebody else gave you in a meeting,

that you have not seen yet. Asking yourself something, or giving yourself a task, is not counted.

Opening the bell marks those as seen, so the number goes away. **Seen is not done** — the work stays in the Leadership Inbox and on your week.

### What it lists

- **New for you** — the items above.
- **Past its date** — open asks and dated meeting tasks whose date has gone. These are listed, never counted.

Each row opens where the work lives: the record an ask came from, or the meeting note for a task (or its day on your week when you may not read the note). The footer links to the **Leadership Inbox** and **Your week**.

### What it does not do

A report being published to you is not a notice. Nothing is emailed, pushed to your phone or sent as a reminder.
