Home tells you what to do next. My Progress answers the slower question: how are my responsibilities going against what is expected?

### What the page shows

- **This week** summary — counts by status, and a meter each for the weekly cycle and the month's cycle, in steps.
- **Needs your attention** — every area not yet done, most pressing first, each with its next step. When everything is done, the page says **Nothing is waiting on you**.
- **This week** and **the month** — each cycle as a row of stations. Select a station to see its detail; select it again to close it.
- **Leadership areas** — every area with its status, cadence and due date. Each row opens the section that owns it.

### Status is worked out, not set

Nothing here can be marked done by hand. A status comes from the records themselves — see [why something is not done yet](topic:my-progress.why-not-done) — and **done means done**: a LifeGroup gathering with attendance taken but no report written is still in progress.

Progress is never a percentage. What you see is what has happened, what is due and what is still open.

Nothing is written on this page — to change a status, do the work where it lives.
