Ticking a ministry during setup records a **claim**. Until an administrator confirms it, it grants nothing. So, for now:

- The ministry is listed under **Other ministries** on Ministries, not with the ones you work in.
- Its page does not say _You serve here_, and you are not offered **New**.
- Reports addressed to a group you claimed do not reach you.

### What to check

1. Open **Setup & walkthrough** from the account menu, or your own page under People. If the ministry shows _awaiting confirmation_, the claim is recorded and waiting.
2. Ask an administrator to look at **Awaiting confirmation** in Administration. Once they confirm it, the ministry opens for you.

If it shows _ended_, an administrator declined it — speak to them. If the ministry is not listed at all, it has not been set up yet.

You cannot confirm your own claim, and nothing on the ministry page can do it for you.

> Leading a ministry is set on the ministry's own record by an administrator, separately from team membership.
