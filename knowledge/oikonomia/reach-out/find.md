Open Reach-Out. The **Reports** tab lists every report, newest date first, split into pages at the bottom.

- **Search reports** finds reports whose title or text contains what you type. It does not search by who wrote them. When nothing fits, the list says **No reports match**.
- The **star** beside a report's date keeps it at the top of the list. Choose it again to unstar.

### A leader's reports

A person's page, under People, lists the Reach-Out reports they started or have contributed to.
