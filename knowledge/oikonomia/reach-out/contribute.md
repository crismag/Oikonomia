Reach-Out is shared work. One leader may lead an effort, another write it up, and a third add what they saw — so every report offers **Edit** to every leader.

### Who is named

- **Reported by** names the leader who started the report. That is where it came from, not who owns it.
- **with** names every other leader who has saved a change to it, in the order they first did. The list shows *with 2 other leaders*.

Only the leader who started a report can delete it.

### Adding to a report

Open it, choose **Edit**, add your part, and choose **Save report**. You are added to the contributors.

If another leader saved the same report while you were writing, the editor says **Another leader added to this report while you were writing.** What you typed is still on screen but not saved. Copy it if you need it, choose **Open their version**, then **Edit** again and add your part to what they wrote.

### Comment or edit?

Edit when you are adding to the account of what happened. Use **Comments** under the report to respond, ask or discuss — a comment never changes the report. Write it in the box, then choose **Comment** (or press Ctrl+Enter).
