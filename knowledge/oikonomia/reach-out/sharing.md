Reach-Out reports are not narrowed by audience. Every leader who can open Reach-Out sees every report in the list, can open it, choose **Edit**, and comment on it.

There is no setting to make a Reach-Out report private, to choose who reads it, or to mark it confidential. Who should see Reach-Out reports has not been decided for the product yet, so nothing on the report pretends to limit it.

You do not need to share a report: other leaders already see it. To draw a particular leader's attention to it, ask them from the report — see [Asks on a Reach-Out report](topic:reach-out.asks).

> Do not write anything here that only some leaders should read. Use a [Leadership Report](destination:leadership-reports) with the right audience instead — see [Who can read a report](topic:reports.visibility).
