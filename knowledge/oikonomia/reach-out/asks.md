### Asking

Under the report is *Does this need anything from leadership?*. Leave it on **No — informational only** unless you need something. Otherwise choose **Please take notice**, **I need an action** or **I need approval**, write what you need, choose who it is **Requested from**, add **Needed by** if there is a date, and **Send request**. Your ask is listed there on the report. See [Ask leadership for something](topic:asks.raise).

### When you are asked

The ask arrives in your [Leadership Inbox](destination:inbox). If it asks you for an **action**, the report also shows an **Asked of you** card above the question, with the request, who asked, and when it is needed by (*Was needed by* once that date has passed).

- **Put on my week** adds the request to your [Weekly Agenda](destination:weekly-agenda) on the needed-by date, or today if that has passed or there is none, and moves the ask to *In progress*.
- It then reads **On your week**, which opens that day.
- **Respond in Leadership Inbox** is where you finish it: **Completed**, or **Can't do it** with a note.

Asks for attention or approval do not appear on the card; answer them in the Leadership Inbox. See [Put an ask on your week](topic:asks.put-on-week).
