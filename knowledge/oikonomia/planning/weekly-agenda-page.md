Weekly Agenda is the week you are running. It has three views of the same items; switching view never changes what exists.

- **Agenda** — each day in order. All-day entries sit above timed ones, and a **To do** list under them holds the day's tasks.
- **List** — every item in the week, grouped by **Day**, **Ministry**, **Type** or **Status** (the **Group** menu appears in this view).
- **Calendar** — the seven days side by side. On a phone the days stack.

### What is on it

- **Entries** are things that happen: an event, a gathering, an activity. They are not ticked off.
- **Tasks** are things to do, with a box to tick. Tasks a meeting gave you appear on their due date, labelled with the meeting — or "From a meeting" if you may not read that note.
- **This week** under the days holds items filed to the week rather than a day, such as follow-ups you put on your week from your own leadership report.

### Opening an item

Open an entry to see it in full, with **Edit** when you may change it, **Duplicate**, and its own tasks. In List and Calendar, a task opens a panel where you can rename it, **Mark done** or **Remove** it; in the Agenda view a task's box only ticks it. A meeting task opens its meeting note when you may read it.

**Search this week…** and **Filter** (by type, or **Hide completed**) narrow the List and Calendar views. **Printable page** lays the week out as a binder page, and **Month** opens [Monthly Calendar](destination:monthly-calendar) at the same week.
