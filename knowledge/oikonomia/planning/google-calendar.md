Oikonomia keeps the church's calendar. Google Calendar can show it, and can show you your own day beside it, but it is not a second place to plan. Both work only where your church has connected **Google Workspace**; otherwise, and on a demonstration, nothing here connects to Google.

### Church events in Google Calendar

When your church has set a church calendar, every event on the calendar and every LifeGroup gathering is published to it, as the church mailbox, shortly after it is saved. Changes and deletions follow; a cancelled gathering is removed, and put back if it is restored. Repeating entries repeat in Google too, without the dates you removed.

- Subscribe to the church calendar in Google Calendar (or any calendar app that reads it) to see church events there.
- It goes one way. Editing or deleting a published event in Google changes nothing in Oikonomia, and the next publish puts it back. Change the event in Oikonomia.
- A gathering shows its venue's name, never an address.
- If Google cannot be reached, your change is still saved. An administrator sees the failure in **Administration → Google Workspace** and can choose **Publish all events** to try again.

### Your Google Calendar on your week

On [Weekly Agenda](destination:weekly-agenda) (Agenda and Calendar views) and in the day panel of [Monthly Calendar](destination:monthly-calendar), your own Google Calendar events appear under **From your Google Calendar**, in a dashed box.

- They are read from your calendar as you, when the page opens, and are not kept in Oikonomia.
- They cannot be opened as entries, ticked or edited here, and they do not count on Home or My Progress. Choose one to open it in Google Calendar.
- Church events Oikonomia published are left out, so nothing appears twice.
- **Google Calendar** in the toolbar shows or hides them. Your choice is remembered on this browser.
- If Oikonomia says your record needs your church Google Workspace address, ask an administrator to put that address on your record in People. A personal Gmail address cannot be read.

Oikonomia does not read anyone else's calendar, and there is no two-way sync.
