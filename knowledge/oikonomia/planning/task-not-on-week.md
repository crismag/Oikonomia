### A task from a meeting

A meeting task reaches a week only when it has **both** someone it is given to and a due date, and it reaches the week of that person. Under each task, the meeting note says what it still needs: _Assign someone and add a due date to put it on a week_, _Add a due date to put it on a week_, or _On your week_ with the day. Ask whoever keeps the note, or add them yourself if you may edit it.

### It is in another week

Check the week shown under the title. Use **Today**, or the arrows, to move. A meeting task sits on its due date, which may not be this week.

### The view is narrowed

In List and Calendar, **Search this week…** and **Filter** hide what does not match; **Hide completed** hides ticked tasks. Clear them, or switch to **Agenda**, which always shows everything.

### It is filed to the week, not a day

Follow-ups you put on your week from your own leadership report have no day. They are under **This week** below the days.

### It is not on Home

Home's **This week** lists at most six things, from today to the end of the week, and leaves out anything ticked. Weekly Agenda has the rest.

### Someone else's task

Tasks are personal. You see your own, and asks or meeting tasks given to you — never another leader's. Entries on the calendar are shared.
