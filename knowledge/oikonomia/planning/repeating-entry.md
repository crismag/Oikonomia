A repeating entry shows a repeat mark beside its title on the week, and its rhythm when you open it.

### Make an entry repeat

In the full form (**More options** when adding, or **Edit**), choose **Repeats**: **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Every two weeks**, **Monthly** or **Yearly**. The pattern follows the **Date** you chose — its weekday, or its day of the month.

### Change some of the dates

1. Open the entry on the date you want to change, and choose **Edit**.
2. Make the change.
3. Under **This entry repeats**, choose:
   - **This occurrence** — only the date you opened (this is selected unless you change it);
   - **This and following** — this date and every one after it;
   - **The whole series** — every date, past and future.
4. Choose **Save**.

### Delete some of the dates

1. Open the entry on the date in question and choose **Delete**.
2. Oikonomia asks **What should be deleted?** Choose **This occurrence only**, **This and everything after**, or **The whole series**. **Cancel** leaves it as it was.

To stop a rhythm from a certain date, delete **This and everything after** from that date.

> A copy made with **Duplicate** does not repeat, even when the original does.
