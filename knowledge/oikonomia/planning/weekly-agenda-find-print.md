### Another week

Use the arrows either side of **Today** to go back or forward a week; **Today** returns to this week. **Month** opens Monthly Calendar on the same week.

### Search, filter and group

These narrow the **List** and **Calendar** views. The **Agenda** view always shows the whole week.

- **Search this week…** — matches words in a title, a ministry's name or a location.
- **Filter** — **Everything**, or one kind: **Task**, **Event**, **Gathering**, **Activity**, **Information**. **Hide completed** hides ticked tasks. The button names what is filtered, so you can tell when something is hidden.
- **Group** (List view only) — by **Day**, **Ministry**, **Type** or **Status**.

When nothing matches, the List says **Nothing matches what you are looking for.** Search and filters reset when you leave the page.

### Print the week

1. Choose **Printable page**. The week is laid out as the binder's Weekly Agenda page, with the week's notes.
2. Choose **Print**.
3. **Back to the week** returns to the workspace.
