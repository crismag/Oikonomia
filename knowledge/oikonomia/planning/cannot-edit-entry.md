The calendar is shared working information: every leader who signs in sees the same entries. Your tasks are different — they are yours, and nobody else sees them on their week.

Because entries are shared, **Edit** and **Delete** appear only on an entry you may change:

- one you added, or one you are the organiser of;
- one **Related to** a ministry you serve in.

An entry that is part of the church's own schedule is not one leader's to change at all. When you may not change an entry, opening it says **This is part of the church's schedule, so it is not yours to change. You can still make your own copy of it.**

### What you can still do

- Choose **Duplicate** to make your own copy, then edit that.
- Add your own tasks to it under **Tasks** — they are yours, not the entry's owner's.
- Ask whoever looks after that schedule to change the original.
