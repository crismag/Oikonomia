Five steps from Home to a week that says what is happening and what you mean to do about it. My Progress counts the week as planned once both are written down.

## Start from Home

Destination: home

Look at **This week** on Home: what is still ahead, each row opening that item. Then choose **Add to the week** at the top.

## Add what is happening

Destination: weekly-agenda

On Weekly Agenda, choose **Add** — beside a day in the Agenda view to start on that day. Type what is happening, add a **Time** if it has one, and choose **Add**. Use **More options** for a place, a ministry or a repeat.

## Write down what you mean to do

Open the entry you added and type under **Tasks** ("Add a task"), then press Enter. The task sits under **To do** on that day. Asks you put on your week from the Leadership Inbox, and dated tasks a meeting gave you, appear here too.

## Tick it off

When it is done, tick the box beside it. It stays on the week, struck through; **Hide completed** in **Filter** hides finished tasks in List and Calendar.

## See the week counted

Destination: my-progress

On My Progress, **Plan the week** is done once something is scheduled this week and you have written down what you mean to do. It is due on the second day of the week.
