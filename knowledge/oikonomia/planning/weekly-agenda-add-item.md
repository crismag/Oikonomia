### Add an entry

1. On [Weekly Agenda](destination:weekly-agenda), choose **Add**. It starts on today if you are looking at this week, otherwise on the first day of that week. In the Agenda view, **Add** under a day starts on that day.
2. Type what is happening. A time is optional.
3. Choose **Add**, or press Enter.

**More options** opens the full form: date, start and end, **All day**, description, location, meeting link, **Related to** a ministry, **Repeats**, and people. What you add is a calendar entry, so it also appears on Monthly Calendar. There is no reminder setting: nothing sends reminders.

### Add a task

Open an entry and type under **Tasks** — "Add a task". The task goes on that entry's day. Tasks also arrive from elsewhere: [Put on my week](topic:asks.put-on-week) from the Leadership Inbox, a [meeting task](topic:meeting-notes.tasks) given to you with a due date, and follow-ups from your own report.

### A day or the week

When you add from Weekly Agenda, the item always has a day. Follow-ups you put on your week from your own leadership report are filed to the week instead, and appear under **This week**, so no deadline is invented.

### Tick it done

Tick the box beside a task. Entries have no box. To rename, move or remove something, see [Change, move or remove something on the week](topic:weekly-agenda.change-item). Ticking a meeting task completes that meeting's own task. Finished tasks stay on the week, struck through; **Hide completed** in the Filter menu hides them.
