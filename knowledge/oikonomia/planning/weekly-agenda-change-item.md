The week holds two kinds of thing, and they change in different places. **Entries** (events, gatherings, activities) are on the calendar. **Tasks** have a box to tick.

### Move or change an entry

1. Open the entry — select it in any view of [Weekly Agenda](destination:weekly-agenda), or on Monthly Calendar.
2. Choose **Edit**.
3. Change the **Date** to move it, or **Start**, **End** or **All day** to change the time. The title, **Description**, **Location or meeting link**, **Meeting link**, **Related to**, **Repeats** and **People** can be changed here too.
4. Choose **Save**.

If the entry repeats, say how far the change reaches — see [repeating entries](topic:planning.repeating-entry).

### Delete an entry

Open it and choose **Delete**. An entry that does not repeat is removed straight away; a repeating one asks which dates to delete first.

**Duplicate** makes a copy on the same day, titled "(copy)" and not repeating. Edit the copy to put it on another day.

No **Edit** or **Delete**? The entry is not yours to change — see [why you cannot edit an entry](topic:planning.cannot-edit-entry).

### Rename or remove a task

1. Switch the week to **List** or **Calendar**. (In the Agenda view, a task's words only tick it.)
2. Select the task. A panel opens.
3. Type in the title to rename it; the change saves when you leave the field.
4. **Mark done** ticks it; **Remove** deletes it.

A task cannot be given a different day. To do it on another day, remove it and add it again under an entry on that day.

### Tick a task done

Tick the box beside it on the week, in an entry's **Tasks**, or in the day panel on Monthly Calendar. Tick again to undo. Ticking records only that you did it; nobody is notified.

### Meeting tasks

A task a meeting gave you is changed in its meeting note: its wording, who has it and its due date belong to the meeting. You can tick it done from the week. Selecting it in List or Calendar opens the note when you may read it.
