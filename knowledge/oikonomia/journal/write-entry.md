1. Open the [Leadership Journal](destination:leadership-journal), under **Organization** in the sidebar.
2. Choose **New entry**. A blank entry opens.
3. Type a title where it says **Untitled entry**. The title is kept when you click away from it.
4. Write below. Press Enter for a new line; use the toolbar to turn the line you are on into a heading, bullet or numbered list, checklist or quote, or to add a divider.
5. Watch the corner: **Saving…**, then **Saved**.

Choose **Leadership** at the top to go back to your list. The entry is under **My development**.

### Worth knowing

- The entry is private to you: _Nobody else can open this, whatever their role._ The browser tab title never shows what you wrote about.
- If you leave with unsaved changes, the browser warns you first.
- If the entry was changed in another tab, Oikonomia keeps what you typed and says so. **Open the other version** replaces your unsaved text with the saved one.
