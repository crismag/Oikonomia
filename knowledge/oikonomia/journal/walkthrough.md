Five steps, from a first private entry to an accountability summary your leader can read.

## Open the Leadership Journal

Destination: leadership-journal

It is under **Organization** in the sidebar. Everyone has one, and nobody else can read yours.

## Write an entry

Choose **New entry**, give it a title, and write. It saves as you go; wait for **Saved** before you close the tab.

## Come back to it

Your entries are listed under **My development**. Open one to add to it.

## Choose what to share

Destination: leadership-journal

When your leader should see part of your reflection, open the entry, choose **Use in a summary**, tick those lines and choose **Draw a summary**. The rest stays private.

## Decide who reads the summary

Destination: leadership-reports

Choose **Open the summary**. It is a private leadership report until you choose its readers, so set them before you publish it.
