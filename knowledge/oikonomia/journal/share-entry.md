A journal entry itself is never shared. What you can share is a **copy of lines you choose**, as a separate leadership report.

1. Open the entry in the [Leadership Journal](destination:leadership-journal).
2. Choose **Use in a summary**. Oikonomia saves the entry first.
3. Under **What belongs in the summary?**, tick the lines to account for.
4. Choose **Draw a summary**.
5. A note says the report was drawn and is **private** until you say who may read it. Choose **Open the summary**.
6. The report opens for editing. Choose its readers there, as for any leadership report — see [Who can read a report](topic:reports.visibility).

### What is shared, and what is not

- Only the ticked lines are copied. Everything unticked stays in the journal.
- The report does not point back to the entry. Whoever reads the report gains no way into your journal, even if you widen its audience later.
- Changing the entry afterwards does not change the report, and changing the report does not change the entry.

**Draw a summary** stays unavailable until you tick at least one line. An entry with no written lines says _Write something first_.
