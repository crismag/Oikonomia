Leadership Reports is under My Work in the sidebar. It has three tabs.

- **My Reports** — reports you wrote.
- **Shared With Me** — reports other leaders wrote that you may read.
- **Documents** — documents registered under Leadership Reports.

Every list shows only reports you may read. When some reports are held to an audience you are not part of, the page gives a count and nothing else — no titles, authors or subjects.

### Finding a report

Search by text, or narrow the list by **Status**, **Type**, **Visibility** and **Ministry**. When your reports came from more than one place (a LifeGroup gathering, a meeting, Reach-Out), chips let you pick a source. Tags under the toolbar filter by tag.

**Sort by** and **Group by** change the order, not what is listed. Grouping (by type, status, category, week or month) shows every match at once; paging applies only when nothing is grouped. The star on a row moves that report to the top for you alone.

Filters live in the address, so Back, a refresh or a shared link keeps them.

### Starting a report

**New report** offers **Write report here** (the binder's editor) or **Link existing document** (the writing stays in a document you already keep). See [Write a leadership report](topic:reports.create).
