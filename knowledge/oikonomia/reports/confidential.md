A report is Confidential only when its author ticks **Confidential**, beside _Who can read this_ in the editor. Nobody else can mark it.

### What it changes

- Anyone else who may read it sees it listed as **Confidential**, but a list never carries its content. They read it by opening the report itself.
- Each time someone other than you opens it, that opening is recorded: who, and when. What the report says is never recorded.
- On your report, **Confidential · who has opened it** lists those openings. Nobody but you sees that list. Your own openings are not recorded.

### What it does not change

It does **not** change who may read the report. That is still _Who can read this_ — see [Who can read a report](topic:reports.visibility). If you want fewer readers, choose Only me or Named people as well.

It also does not give anyone extra access: oversight and administration still do not open it.
