Six steps, from an empty report to one your leaders can read.

## Open Leadership Reports

Destination: leadership-reports

Leadership Reports is under My Work in the sidebar.

## Start a report

Choose **New report**, then **Write report here**. The report is created as a Draft and opens in the editor.

## Say what it is

Type a title, then set **Type**, **About**, **Related to** and **Period** in the row below it. Only the title matters for finding it again; the rest help your readers.

## Choose who can read it

In **Who can read this**, pick Only me, Named people, Leadership or Shared. For Named people or Shared, choose the people under _Also visible to_. Tick **Confidential** only if the report must be handled that way. [Who can read a report](topic:reports.visibility) explains each choice.

## Write it

Write in the document. Mark a line as **Follow-up** if it is something you need to do; you can put it on your week later. It saves as you type.

## Publish it

Choose **Published** at the bottom of the editor or at the top of the report. Its content becomes the submitted record, and discussion stays open. If you need a response, answer _Does this need anything from leadership?_ under the report.
