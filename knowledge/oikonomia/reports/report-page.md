### The header

The title, then type, period and stage. A category is shown when it is not general; where the report came from (a gathering, a meeting, Reach-Out) links back there. **Confidential** appears when the author marked it. The lock label shows _Who can read this_ — select it to see who can reach the report. Below: the author, and who or what it is about.

Buttons on the right: **Print** for everyone; **Edit** for the author while the content can still change; and the stages the author may move it to. See [Report stages](topic:reports.statuses).

If you wrote a confidential report, **Confidential · who has opened it** sits under the header.

### Tabs

- **Report** — the content, or for a linked report, the document that holds it.
- **Discussion** — comments. Discussion may be closed or limited to selected people; it stays open after publishing.
- **Documents** — the report's content document and supporting documents.
- **Activity** — what happened to the report and when.

### Under the report

- **Follow-ups in this report** — the author's own open follow-ups, with **Put on my week**. See [Follow-ups](topic:reports.follow-ups).
- **Asked of you** — actions someone asked of you from this report, with **Put on my week**. See [Put an ask on your week](topic:asks.put-on-week).
- **Does this need anything from leadership?** — ask for attention, an action or an approval. See [Ask leadership for something](topic:asks.raise).
