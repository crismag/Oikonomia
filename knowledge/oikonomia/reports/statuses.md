A report moves through stages your church configures. The standard ones:

- **Draft** — where every new report starts.
- **Shared** — out to its audience, still open to changes by its author.
- **Published** — the submitted record. Its content no longer changes.
- **Archived** — kept, and no longer current.

Only the author moves a report. The buttons at the top of the report (and at the bottom of the editor) are the stages the author may move it to from where it is.

### What publishing freezes

Moving to Published or Archived keeps a copy of the content as it stood and stamps the date. After that nobody can edit the content — the page says it is the submitted record. **Discussion continues**: comments stay open if the report allowed them.

### Reopening

The author can move a Published report back to Draft or Shared to correct it. The copy made when it was published is kept, so a correction never quietly replaces what leadership read.

An Archived report is not reopened from the report page.

> A stage is not a review. Nobody approves a report. It goes to the people _Who can read this_ names, as information. Moving between stages does not change who may read it.
