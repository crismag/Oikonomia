A follow-up is something you, the author, need to do about what you wrote.

1. Open your report and choose **Edit** (available while it is a Draft or Shared).
2. Put the cursor on the line and choose **Follow-up** in the toolbar. The line is set apart as a follow-up.
3. Choose **Done**.
4. Under the report, **Follow-ups in this report** lists your open follow-ups. Choose **Put on my week** beside one.

It goes on the [Weekly Agenda](destination:weekly-agenda) for this week, in the week's notes rather than on a day, so nothing gets a deadline you did not choose. The button becomes **On your week**, which opens that week. Once the agenda item is completed, the follow-up offers Put on my week again.

You mark lines while the report can still be edited; the card and **Put on my week** stay available after you publish.

### What it does not do

- Only the author sees this card. Readers see the follow-up line in the text, and nothing lands on their week.
- It does not ask anybody for anything. To ask another leader, use _Does this need anything from leadership?_ — see [Ask leadership for something](topic:asks.raise).
