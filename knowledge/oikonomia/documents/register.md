Registering records a document that lives somewhere else — Google Drive, a website, a shared folder. Nothing is copied into Oikonomia, and uploading a file is not possible.

### Steps

1. Open **Documents & Forms** and choose **Register document**.
2. **What is it called?** — the name you would look for it by. Required.
3. **Where is it?** — paste the full address. Required. Oikonomia shows whether it will be filed as Google Drive or as a Link, read from the address alone; it does not open or check the document.
4. **What kind of thing is it?** — Document, Spreadsheet, Plan, Report, Checklist, Schedule, Announcement or Link.
5. **Which ministry does it belong to?** — optional. Choosing one files it on that ministry's Documents view.
6. **Anything worth saying about it?** — optional.
7. Choose **Register document**.

It appears under **Reference documents** and in Resource Search.

### Worth knowing

- The document's own sharing still decides who can open it. Registering it does not share it.
- Oikonomia does not connect to Google Drive. Copy the link from Drive and paste it.
- If a required field is missing or the save is refused, the form stays open with what you typed and says what to fix.
