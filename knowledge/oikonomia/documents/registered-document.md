A document that lives somewhere else — a link, or a file in Google Drive — has a page of its own in the binder. It shows the binder's record of the document, not the document itself. Choose a document's name on a ministry's **Documents** view, in Documents & Forms or in Resource Search to open it.

### What the page shows

- The title, what kind of thing it is, and where it lives (**Google Drive** or **Link**).
- A description, if one was given.
- **Where it lives** — the web address. Where Google Drive is connected and Drive lets you see the file, who owns it and when it last changed.
- **Filed under** — each ministry or record it is filed in. Choose one to go there. Only places you may see are listed.
- Who registered it, and when.

### Opening it

**Open** (or **Open in Drive**) opens the document where it lives, in a new tab. Whoever keeps the document decides whether it opens for you: filing it in the binder does not share it. Only ordinary web addresses are opened.

### Changing it

People who work in the ministry it is filed in — or whoever registered it, if it is filed in no ministry — also see **Edit details** and **Unfile from** each place. See [Edit or unfile a document](topic:documents.edit-unfile).

### Nothing here to open

If the page says _There is nothing here to open_, the document is no longer in the binder, or it is not one you may see.
