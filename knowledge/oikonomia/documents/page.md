Both pages are under **Library** in the sidebar.

### Documents & Forms

Two different things live here:

- **Ministry forms** — forms and checklists built in Oikonomia. The newest few are listed; **Open form library** shows them all. **Create form** starts one. See [Forms](topic:forms.page).
- **Reference documents** — documents that live elsewhere and have been registered in the binder. **Register document** adds one; **Open** takes you to where it lives; **Open document index** opens Resource Search.

Documents are links, not uploads. The binder records what a document is and where it is. It does not store files, and whoever keeps the document still decides who may open it.

The **Volunteer Forms** panel is a placeholder note: that section has not been organised yet.

### Resource Search

For "I know we have this somewhere". Type in **Search resources…**, then narrow with **Filters**: section of the binder (Ministry, Meeting Notes, Leadership Reports and so on), what it is related to, tags, or who added it. Sort by relevance, recently updated, or title.

Each result shows where it is filed and who added it, and **Open** takes you to it. Only material you may discover is listed.

The search is kept in the page address, so Back works and a search can be bookmarked.
