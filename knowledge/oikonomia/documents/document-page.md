Some documents are written in the binder rather than kept elsewhere. A ministry's **Plan**, **Report**, **Announcement** or **Checklist** is one of these. It belongs to the ministry; the line under the title says its kind, the ministry, who started it and when it last changed.

### Starting one

On the ministry's page, choose **New**, then **Plan**, **Report**, **Announcement** or **Checklist** under **Create in the binder**. The new document opens for writing. See [Ministries](topic:ministries.page).

### Writing

The ministry's lead and the people on its team can write. Type a title, then write below, using the toolbar for headings, lists, checklists, quotes and dividers. It saves as you go (**Saving…**, **Saved**); **Save now** saves straight away.

If someone else in the ministry changed it while you were writing, the page says so and keeps what you typed unsaved. **Open their version** shows theirs; you will need to add your part again.

### Reading only

If the page says _Writing here is for the people who work in this ministry_, you can read it but not change it — for example because the ministry is shared with you rather than yours.

### Deleting

Writers see **Delete document** at the bottom. Oikonomia asks first, then returns you to the ministry.

### Registered documents

A **registered** document lives somewhere else, such as Google Drive. Its page shows the binder's record of it and **Open**, rather than a place to write; see [A registered document's page](topic:documents.registered-document).

### Nothing here to open

The document is no longer in the binder, or it is not one you may see.
