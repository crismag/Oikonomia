Files always live in Google Drive. Oikonomia keeps a record of each one — its name, where it opens, which ministry it belongs to — and never a copy of the file.

This works only where your church has connected its Google Workspace. Where it is not connected, **Add from Drive** asks you to paste the document's link instead; see [Add a link to a ministry](topic:ministries.add-link).

### Choose a file

1. Open **Ministry** and choose the ministry.
2. Choose **New**, then **Add from Drive**.
3. Look in **Ministry folder**, **My Drive** or **Shared with me**, or type in the search box. Choose a folder to open it; the path above the list takes you back.
4. Choose the file. It is filed on the ministry's **Documents** view.

Choosing the same file again, or from another ministry, files the same record there too; it is never listed twice.

### Upload a file

In the same panel, choose **Upload file** and pick a file of up to 25 MB. It goes into the ministry's folder in Drive and is filed on the ministry. Upload larger files in Drive itself, then choose them here.

### Start a Google Doc, Sheet or Slides

Choose **New Google Doc**, **New Google Sheet** or **New Google Slides**, give it a name, then **Create in the ministry folder**. It opens in a new tab and is filed on the ministry.

### The ministry folder

Each ministry has one folder in the church's Drive, made the first time someone uploads or starts a file there. Until then the **Ministry folder** tab says it has none yet. If your church has not named a Drive folder for ministries, uploading and starting files are not offered; choosing from My Drive still works.

### On document lists

A ministry's **Documents** view and **Reference documents** in Documents & Forms show, for Drive documents, who owns the file and when it last changed, and **Open in Drive**. The document's name opens [its page in the binder](topic:documents.registered-document), which shows the same.

### Worth knowing

- Oikonomia opens Drive **as you**. You see only what Drive lets you see, and Drive's own sharing decides who can open a file. Filing it in a ministry does not share it.
- Uploading and starting files are for the ministry's lead and team. If the ministry is only shared with you, you can still choose a file.
- If Oikonomia says your person record needs your church Google address, ask an administrator to add your church email to your person record.
- A document with no Drive details on the list is one Drive would not show you, or one that has been removed from Drive.
