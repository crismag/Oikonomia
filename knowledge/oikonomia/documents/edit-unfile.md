These change the binder's record only. The document itself — in Google Drive or at its link — is never renamed, moved or deleted from here.

### Edit details

1. Open the document's page: choose its name on the ministry's **Documents** view, in Documents & Forms or in Resource Search.
2. Choose **Edit details**.
3. Change the **Title**, **Kind**, **Web address** or **Description**. The address must start with `https://` or `http://`.
4. Choose **Save details**. If something needs fixing, the form stays open and says what.

Changing the address points the record at a different document. It is read again as Google Drive or Link, and what Drive said about the old file is no longer shown.

### Unfile it from a place

1. On the document's page, under **Filed under**, choose **Unfile from** beside the place.
2. Oikonomia asks first. Choose **Unfile it**.

It stops being listed there. It stays filed anywhere else it is filed, and stays in Documents & Forms and Resource Search.

### Who can do this

- A document filed in a ministry: that ministry's lead and the people on its team. If the ministry is only shared with you, you can open the document but not change it.
- A document filed in no ministry: whoever registered it.

If you do not see **Edit details**, you are not one of these. A Plan, Report, Announcement or Checklist written in the binder always stays in its ministry; see [A ministry document](topic:documents.document-page).
