Signing in proves who you are. What you see — a ministry's information, reports addressed to leadership — follows from where you serve, and that is an **assignment** the church confirms.

### Saying where you serve

Open **Setup & walkthrough** from the account menu (it also runs when you first sign in). On **Your ministries** and **Teams and committees**, tick the ones you are part of.

Each tick is saved straight away as a **claim**, shown as _awaiting confirmation_. A claim grants nothing: no membership, no readership. It waits in Administration for an administrator to confirm or decline. You cannot confirm your own claim.

If your ministry or group is not on the list, ask an administrator to add it.

### When a record about you is wrong

On **Your responsibilities**, choose **This is wrong** beside the assignment. That marks it _correction requested_ for an administrator to look at. It does not remove you from anything by itself.

### Seeing where things stand

Your responsibilities, and their state, are listed on your own page under People and on the last screen of setup. An assignment that has ended is kept as history rather than deleted.
