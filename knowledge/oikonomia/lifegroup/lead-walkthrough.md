Six steps, from a gathering that needs a leader to a report Home counts as done.

## Open the schedule

Destination: lifegroups

**LifeGroup** is under **Shared** in the sidebar. Find the evening you will lead. If it is not there, choose **Add schedule** and set its date.

## Put your name on it

In the **You** column, choose **Assign to me** if nobody leads it, or **Add me** to lead alongside someone. Only an assigned leader can record the gathering afterwards.

## Set when and where

In the row, set the **Time** and choose the venue under **Where** from the suggestions. On a phone, **Open** the gathering and use **Edit details**.

## Record who came

After the gathering, **Open** it. Under **Attendance**, tap the people who signed up and came (or **All came**), and use **Add someone by name** for anyone else.

## Write it up

Type the **Exhortation** topic. Add entries under **Sharing and notes**, choosing who can read each one. Add anything else under **Leader's summary**. Everything saves as you type.

## Complete the gathering

Choose **Complete gathering**. Home and My Progress now count the gathering report as done. If something needs leadership's attention, use **Does this need anything from leadership?** on the same page. You can **Reopen** it later.
