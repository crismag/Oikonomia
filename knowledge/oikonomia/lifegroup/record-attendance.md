Attendance is recorded on the gathering itself, by a leader assigned to it.

### Steps

1. Open **LifeGroup** and **Open** the gathering.
2. Under **Attendance**, if people signed up for this gathering, they are listed as *signed up and not yet marked*. Tap each person who came, or choose **All came**.
3. For anyone else who came, type their name in **Add someone by name** and choose **Add** (or press Enter). They are marked **Present**.
4. To change a mark, choose **Present**, **Absent** or **Excused** on that person's row.

Marks save as you make them. The heading counts them — for example *7 of 10 signed up · 2 more came*, or simply *9 present*.

### Worth knowing

- A name added by hand is recorded as that name. It is not linked to a person in People, so it does not appear in their attendance history.
- Attendance is the only thing Oikonomia names as missing before you complete the gathering, but completing is never blocked.
- Recording attendance alone does not finish the gathering: Home keeps it as in progress until the report is complete. See [Write up and complete a gathering](topic:lifegroup.write-up).
- What you mark is what [Attendance](destination:attendance) adds up.
