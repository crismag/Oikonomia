**Attendance** is under **Organization** in the sidebar. It adds up the marks leaders made at their gatherings. Nothing is entered on this page.

### What it shows

- **Recorded participation** — one row per gathering that has attendance marked, newest first: the venue, the day, the campus, how many were present and *of* how many were marked.
- The heading totals those present across the rows you are looking at, and how many were there for the first time.
- **Search occasions** narrows by venue or day.

Choose a row to open that gathering.

### What it does not show

- **Gatherings nobody marked.** A gathering with no marks is not counted as zero; it is left out.
- **Services and events.** Only LifeGroup gatherings record attendance today. Sunday services and calendar events record no participation, so nothing is shown for them.

### To change a mark

Open the gathering from its row. Only a leader assigned to that gathering can change attendance. See [Record attendance](topic:lifegroup.record-attendance).
