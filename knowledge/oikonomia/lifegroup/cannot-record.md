On a gathering you do not lead, **Attendance**, **Exhortation**, **Sharing and notes** and **Leader's summary** are read-only, and there is no **Complete gathering**. That is by design: recording what happened belongs to the leaders who were there.

### If you led it

Put your name on it first. On **LifeGroup**, use **Assign to me** (nobody leading) or **Add me** (others already leading). Then open the gathering again. See [Take or give back a gathering](topic:lifegroup.claim).

### If Assign to me and Add me are not offered

The gathering is **Completed** or **Cancelled**. Who led a written-up gathering is part of its record, so names cannot be added afterwards. Ask the leader who completed it to **Reopen** it if something is missing — only its assigned leaders can reopen.

### Campus oversight

Campus oversight may move a gathering, change its venue and name its leaders, but still cannot mark its attendance or write it up unless they are assigned to it.

### Changing the date, time or venue

That is a different right: the gathering's leaders and campus oversight, or any leader while nobody has claimed it. Otherwise you see *This gathering is not yours to change.*
