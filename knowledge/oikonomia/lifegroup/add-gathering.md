Any leader may add a gathering. It is shared with everyone the moment it is added — there is no draft.

### Steps

1. Open **LifeGroup**.
2. Choose **Add schedule** (beside **The schedule**, or in the empty schedule).
3. A new row appears, dated one week after the last gathering on the schedule. It has no time, no place and nobody leading it, so its stage is **Needs leader**.
4. On a wide screen, correct the **Date**, set the **Time** and choose **Where** in the row itself. Changes save when you leave the field.
5. If you will lead it, choose **Assign to me**.

Adding several rows in a row keeps stepping a week further on, so a month can be laid out quickly.

### Worth knowing

- Adding a row does not make it yours. Until someone takes it, anyone may fill in its date, time and place.
- On a phone the rows are stacked and are not edited in place. **Open** the gathering and use **Edit details**. While nobody has claimed it, you can save its date, time and place without leading it.
- If the same evening was added twice, open the extra row and choose **Cancel gathering**. See [Cancel or restore a gathering](topic:lifegroup.cancel). Use **Add me** to join a gathering that already exists rather than adding another row.
