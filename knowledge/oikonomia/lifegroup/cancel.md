A gathering that will not take place is **cancelled**, not deleted. It stays on the schedule marked **Cancelled**, so the roster still shows what was planned — and a row cancelled by mistake can be put back.

### Cancel a gathering

1. Open **LifeGroup** and **Open** the gathering.
2. Choose **Cancel gathering** in the header.
3. Oikonomia asks you to confirm. Choose **Cancel the gathering**, or **Keep it** to leave it as it was.

The page then says the gathering was cancelled, and its stage reads **Cancelled** on the schedule.

### Restore a cancelled gathering

1. Open the cancelled gathering.
2. Choose **Restore gathering**.

It goes back to **Assigned** if someone is leading it, or **Needs leader** if nobody is.

### Who may cancel or restore

The gathering's assigned leaders and campus oversight. While nobody has claimed a row, any leader may cancel it — that is how a duplicate added while preparing the week is tidied away — and restore it again.

### What cancelling changes

- Nothing is owed on it: it no longer appears on Home or My Progress as a gathering to report.
- Nothing can be recorded on it — attendance, exhortation, notes or the summary — until it is restored. Anything already recorded is kept.
- Nobody can **Assign to me** or **Add me** on it, and **Edit details** is not offered until it is restored.

### Worth knowing

- A gathering that has been completed cannot be cancelled: it happened. If the write-up was a mistake, a leader of it can **Reopen** it first.
