Any leader may put their own name on a gathering. It is done from the **You** column of the schedule (or the buttons under each row on a phone). Home's **LifeGroup** card also offers **Assign to me** on a gathering nobody leads.

### The three actions

- **Assign to me** — take a gathering nobody is leading yet. Its stage becomes **Assigned**.
- **Add me** — stand beside the leaders already on it. Use this to co-lead.
- **Step away** — shown beside **You're assigned**. Takes your name off. If you were the only leader, the row goes back to **Needs leader** so somebody else can pick it up.

### What being assigned means

You become responsible for the gathering, and you are the one who can record its attendance and write it up. It does not make the gathering private or yours alone — it stays on the shared schedule. Home lists it under what needs you this week until its report is complete.

### What you cannot do here

- Put **someone else's** name on a gathering, or take theirs off. That belongs to campus oversight; ask them.
- Join or step away from a gathering that is **Completed** or **Cancelled**. Who led a written-up gathering is part of its record.
