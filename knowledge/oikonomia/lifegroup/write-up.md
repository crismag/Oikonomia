After [attendance](topic:lifegroup.record-attendance), the rest of the write-up is on the same page. Only the gathering's assigned leaders can fill it in. What you type saves as you type — there is no Save button.

### Exhortation

Under **Exhortation**, type the **Topic**. **Scripture or reference** and **Short notes** are optional; a topic on its own is complete.

### Leader's summary

Under **Leader's summary**, add anything the records above do not already say. It is optional.

### Complete

Choose **Complete gathering**. The stage becomes **Completed**, the section shows **Report complete**, and it says who completed it. Nothing is sent anywhere and nobody is asked to approve it. If attendance has not been marked, the page says so, but you can still complete.

### Reopen

On a completed gathering, choose **Reopen** to change it. Your summary is kept; the gathering is no longer counted as complete until you choose **Complete gathering** again.

### What Home counts

For a gathering you lead this week, Home and My Progress list **Report the gathering you led** with the steps *Record who came*, *Record the exhortation* and *Complete the gathering report*. It is done only when the report is complete — attendance alone leaves it in progress.
