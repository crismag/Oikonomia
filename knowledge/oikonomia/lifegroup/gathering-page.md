A gathering's page is the record of one evening. The header shows the day and time, the venue, who is leading, the host if the venue has one, and the stage.

Anyone signed in can open a gathering. **Only a leader assigned to this gathering can record it** — everyone else reads what has been recorded.

### From top to bottom

- **Attendance** — who came. See [Record attendance](topic:lifegroup.record-attendance).
- **Exhortation** — what was taught: a topic, and optionally the scripture and short notes.
- **Sharing and notes** — entries from the evening, each with its own audience. See [Who can read a gathering's notes](topic:lifegroup.entry-visibility).
- **Leader's summary** — anything you want to add, then **Complete gathering**. See [Write up and complete a gathering](topic:lifegroup.write-up).
- **Reports** — **Add report** for something that belongs outside the gathering's notes, such as a pastoral concern. See [Write a report from a gathering](topic:lifegroup.gathering-report).
- **Does this need anything from leadership?** — ask for attention, an action or an approval. See [Ask leadership for something](topic:asks.raise).

### Buttons in the header

- **Edit details** — change the date, times, venue and leaders. Shown to the gathering's leaders and campus oversight, and to anyone while nobody has claimed it — though saving needs someone leading it, so take it with **Assign to me** first.
- **Printable view** — a clean sheet of the record, with **Print**. Notes you may not read are left off and counted instead.
