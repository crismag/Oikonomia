A gathering's notes are read by its leaders. Some things should reach fewer people. For those, write a report.

### Steps

1. On the gathering, under **Reports**, choose **Add report**.
2. **Give it a short title**.
3. Choose **What kind of information**.
4. Tick **Confidential** if it should be visible only to you and the people you add. Nobody else will see it, or see that it exists.
5. Choose **Start the report**. The report opens for writing in [Leadership Reports](destination:leadership-reports).

### Worth knowing

- The report is kept with your other Leadership Reports. The gathering is where it came from, not where it lives.
- The gathering's **Reports** list shows only reports you are allowed to read.
- There is no "about this person" field on purpose. Name whoever it concerns in your own words, in the report.
- A report does not ask anyone to do anything. If leadership needs to act, use **Does this need anything from leadership?** on the gathering. See [Ask leadership for something](topic:asks.raise).
