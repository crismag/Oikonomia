A new Oikonomia is empty: no campuses, ministries or people until you add them. Until the church is entered, an administrator's Home shows **Set up your church**, with how many of five steps are done.

### The five steps

1. **Add your campus** — where the church meets. A church with one site has one campus.
2. **Add the ministries** — the areas leaders serve in, so reports and goals have somewhere to belong.
3. **Add the leaders** — the people who will use Oikonomia, with who each reports to.
4. **Invite them in** — an invitation gives someone a way to sign in, and nothing else. See [Invite someone](topic:administration.invite).
5. **Confirm where they serve** — when invited leaders first sign in they say where they serve. [Confirming it](topic:assignments.confirm) decides what each one sees.

The next step to do shows a sentence of explanation and **Go to Administration**, which opens the right section.

### How steps are ticked

Each step is ticked by the records existing — a campus, a ministry, another person, someone else with an account, a confirmed assignment. There is nothing to tick by hand. When all five are done, the card goes away.

The leadership cycle items further down Home still apply to you while you set up.
