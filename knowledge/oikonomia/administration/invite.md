### Invite several people at once

1. In **Administration**, go to **Invite people**.
2. List their email addresses — one per line, or separated by commas.
3. Choose **Send invitations** (or **Create accounts** where this installation cannot send email).

Each address gets its own result:

- _Invitation sent_ — they get an email with a link to set a password.
- _Account created — no email sent_ — the installation cannot send email; see below.
- _Already signs in_ — left alone.
- _Not an email address_ or _Held by another record_ — kept in the box to correct and send again.

Someone who is not in **People** yet is added, recorded under their address. The first time they sign in, Welcome asks their name; after that, their name is changed by an administrator like the rest of their record. _· added to People_ marks them in the results.

### Invite one person from their record

1. In **Administration → People**, choose **Edit** on the person.
2. Enter their **Email address** and choose **Save**.
3. Choose **Edit** again, then **Invite to Oikonomia**.

### The link

It works once and expires after 7 days. If it expires, they can ask for a new one with **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen.

### What an invitation gives

A way to sign in, and nothing else — no ministry, no group, no capability. When they first sign in they say where they serve, and you [confirm it](topic:assignments.confirm).

### When email is not set up

The accounts are still created, and you are told nothing was sent. Ask your installation's operator to set a password for them, then pass it on yourself.

### Worth knowing

- An email address already used by someone else's account is refused.
- To take access away, choose **Deactivate**. Their sessions end straight away; what they wrote stays.
- At most 200 addresses at a time.
- In the public demo, editing people and inviting are switched off, and no email is sent.
