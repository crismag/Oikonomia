The person must already be in **People** in Administration.

### Steps

1. In **Administration → People**, choose **Edit** on the person.
2. Enter their **Email address** and choose **Save**. The invitation goes to this address.
3. Choose **Edit** again, then **Invite to Oikonomia**.

If email is working, you are told _An invitation was sent_. The message contains a link to set a password. The link works once and expires after 15 minutes; if it expires, they can ask for a new one from the sign-in screen.

### What an invitation gives

A way to sign in, and nothing else — no ministry, no group, no capability. When they first sign in they say where they serve, and you [confirm it](topic:assignments.confirm).

### When email is not set up

The account is still created, and you are told this installation cannot send email. Ask your installation's operator to set a password for them, then pass it on yourself.

### Worth knowing

- An email address already used by someone else's account is refused.
- To take access away, choose **Deactivate**. Their sessions end straight away; what they wrote stays.
- In the public demo, editing people and inviting are switched off, and no email is sent.
