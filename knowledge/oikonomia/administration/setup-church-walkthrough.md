Five steps, in the order Home's **Set up your church** card asks for them. Each is ticked on Home once the records exist.

## Add your campus

Destination: administration.campuses

In **Campuses**, enter a **Name** and **City**, then choose **Add campus**. One site means one campus.

## Add the ministries

Destination: administration.ministries

In **Ministries**, enter a **Name**, **Purpose** and **Campus**. Choose a **Lead** if you know it — a ministry can be added without one. Choose **Add ministry** for each.

## Add the leaders

Destination: administration.people

In **People**, enter each leader's **Name**, **What they are called here**, **What they may do**, **Campus** and **Reports to**, then **Add person**. The name they are called here grants nothing; what they may do is their access role.

## Invite them in

Destination: administration.invite

In **Invite people**, list their email addresses and choose **Send invitations**. Anyone not yet in People is added and gives their name when they first sign in. If your installation cannot send email, the accounts are still created and you are told so — ask your installation's operator to set their passwords.

## Confirm where they serve

Destination: administration.assignments

When leaders first sign in, they tick the ministries and groups they serve in. Those claims wait in **Awaiting confirmation**. Choose **Confirm** for each one that is right. Until then, a leader sees none of that ministry's information.
