Oikonomia is a **leadership binder** for church leaders. It is where you plan your week, write down what was said and decided, record outreach, report on your work and see what other leaders have asked of you.

It is not a membership database, a Sunday service planner, a ticket queue or a file store.

The name is the New Testament word for stewardship — looking after a household that has been entrusted to you.

### The question it answers

Everything is arranged around **what needs my attention?** Home shows what needs you now, My Progress shows how your responsibilities are going, and each record lives in one place that everything else links to.

A report reaching you is information. It becomes work only when its author explicitly asks something of you.

### How the church is organised

Oikonomia models the church as campuses, ministries within them, groups such as LifeGroup gatherings, and the people who serve. Where you serve comes from **assignments your church confirms** — signing in proves who you are and nothing more.
