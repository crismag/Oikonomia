Everything below is in the sidebar. A page you do not see there is not part of your work yet — see [Why can't I see a report or note?](topic:permissions.not-found).

### Start of the day

- **See what needs you** — [Home](destination:home). Overdue work, what leaders asked of you, and this week.
- **See how your cycle is going** — [My Progress](destination:my-progress).
- **See what leaders asked of you** — [Leadership Inbox](destination:inbox).

### Plan

- **Plan the week, tick things off** — [Weekly Agenda](destination:weekly-agenda). See [Add something to the week](topic:weekly-agenda.add-item).
- **Add an event or see the month** — [Monthly Calendar](destination:monthly-calendar).
- **Put an ask you were given on a day** — [Put an ask on your week](topic:asks.put-on-week).

### Write things down

- **Take notes or minutes of a meeting** — [Meeting Notes](destination:meeting-notes).
- **Record pastoral outreach** — [Reach-Out](destination:reach-out).
- **Report on your work to your leaders** — [Leadership Reports](destination:leadership-reports). See [Write a leadership report](topic:reports.create).
- **Write for yourself** — reflection and development in [Leadership Journal](destination:leadership-journal).
- **Set or update this year's goals** — [Goals](destination:goals). See [Whose a goal is](topic:goals.scope).

### Work with others

- **Schedule, lead or record a LifeGroup gathering** — [LifeGroup](destination:lifegroups).
- **See a ministry's goals, links and people** — [Ministry](destination:ministries).
- **Ask another leader for attention, an action or an approval** — from the report or note itself. See [Ask leadership for something](topic:asks.raise).
- **Read reports that reached you** — [Reports to you](destination:reports-to-you), or **Shared With Me** in [Leadership Reports](destination:leadership-reports.shared).

### Find things

- **A document or form** — [Documents & Forms](destination:documents).
- **Anything, by a word** — [Resource Search](destination:resource-search), or the search box at the top of every page.
- **A person** — [People](destination:people).

### You

- **Your password, signed-in devices, and how Oikonomia looks** — [Account & security](destination:account-security). See [Change how Oikonomia looks](topic:account.appearance).
- **Say where you serve** — [Saying where you serve](topic:assignments.claims).
