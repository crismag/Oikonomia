The sidebar is arranged as a binder, in two contexts.

### My Binder — your own work

- **Home** — Home and My Progress: where you stand.
- **My Work** — Weekly Agenda, Monthly Calendar, Meeting Notes, Reach-Out, Leadership Reports and Goals.
- **Shared** — LifeGroup and Ministry, where a team works on the same records.
- **Library** — Documents & Forms, and Resource Search.

### Leadership — seeing how others are doing

- **Oversight** — Leadership Inbox, Team Overview and Reports to you.
- **Organization** — People, Attendance, Campuses, Leadership Journal and Administration.

### Why some items are missing

**Campuses** appears only to people holding campus oversight, and **Administration** only to people holding administration. Everything else is listed for everyone, but what each page shows still depends on what your church has confirmed about where you serve.

A hidden link is a convenience, not the protection: the server refuses what you may not do whether or not a link is drawn. See [Capabilities and assignments](topic:permissions.capabilities).
