Five stops, in the order a leader usually meets them. Nothing here changes anything.

## Start at Home

Destination: home

Home shows what needs your attention, what other leaders have asked of you, what is left in the week and what you are carrying. Open any row to continue it where it lives — Home keeps no second copy.

## Plan the week

Destination: weekly-agenda

Weekly Agenda is your working week, shown as Agenda, List or Calendar. Add what you intend to do. Dated tasks given to you in a meeting appear here on their day.

## Write a leadership report

Destination: leadership-reports

Leadership Reports is your account of your work, addressed to an audience you choose. Publishing puts it in front of those readers; it does not put it in anybody's queue unless you ask for a response.

## See what has been asked of you

Destination: inbox

The Leadership Inbox lists what leaders have actually asked of you — take notice, act or approve. An action you take on can be put on your week from its row.

## Find help while you work

The Guide lists help for the page you have open, and you can browse or search the rest. The setup and tour you saw on your first visit is always available again from the account menu, under **Setup & walkthrough**.
