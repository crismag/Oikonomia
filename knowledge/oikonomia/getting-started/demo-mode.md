The public demo is Oikonomia itself — real pages and real permissions — running on invented data. A **DEMO** bar across the top of every page tells you that you are in it.

### Getting in

There is no password. Choose a person to **explore as**, or, where offered, **Try it as yourself** with just a name. You see and can do exactly what that person could. Switch people from the name in the demo bar.

### Shared, and reset

Everyone uses the same data, so other visitors may be changing the records you are looking at. What you do is saved until the next refresh, when everything returns to how it started and temporary profiles are removed. The bar shows when the next refresh is, and you are warned shortly before it. After a refresh you are returned to the sign-in screen. **About this demo** in the bar has the details.

### Switched off in the demo

- Email: sign-in links, password resets and invitations.
- Google sign-in and other outside services.
- Signing in with a password, signing other devices out, and adding people or changing their name, email or access role.
- Configuration, backups, exports and retention can be seen but not changed.

Church work — reports, notes, the week, gatherings, assignments — works as it does in a church's own installation.
