On your first visit Oikonomia takes you through a short setup before Home. You can run it again at any time from the account menu, under **Setup & walkthrough**.

One question per screen, with **Back** and **Continue**:

1. **Welcome** — what this is for.
2. **Your details** — your name, what you are called here, your campus and what you may do in Oikonomia. These are the church's records, so an administrator changes them.
3. **Your responsibilities** — everywhere the church has recorded that you serve. Use **This is wrong** to ask for a correction.
4. **Your ministries** and **Teams and committees** — tick the ones you are part of.
5. **Reporting** — who you report to and who reports to you.
6. **Your workspace is ready** — a summary, and a quick tour of the pages you can reach.

Steps with nothing to show are left out.

If you were invited by email alone, Welcome first asks **Your name**. You give it once; after that an administrator changes it.

### Ticking is a claim

Ticking a ministry or group records that you said so. **It grants nothing** until an administrator confirms it, and you cannot confirm your own. See [Claiming where you serve](topic:assignments.claims).

### Leaving part-way

Progress is saved as you go. **Save and exit** returns you to Home; **Go to my workspace** on the last screen finishes setup.
