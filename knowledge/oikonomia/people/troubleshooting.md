### I cannot find them

- Clear the search and choose **All campuses** — a campus filter stays on until you change it, and it is kept in the page address.
- Search matches the name and what they are called here (Pastor, LifeGroup leader), not their ministry.
- If they are still not listed, they have not been added to the directory. Ask your church's administrator.

### Their role, campus or reporting leader is wrong

Nobody corrects a person from People or their page. An administrator changes it in Administration. _No reporting leader recorded_ means nobody has entered one yet.

### A responsibility shows as awaiting confirmation

A claim to serve somewhere counts for nothing until an administrator confirms it. See [Saying where you serve](topic:assignments.claims).

### Their page shows little of their work

A person's page lists only what you may already read. Reports whose audience does not include you, and other leaders' personal meeting notes, are not shown — see [Why can't I see a report or note?](topic:permissions.not-found). Where work involving them is closed to you, the page gives a count, never the records.
