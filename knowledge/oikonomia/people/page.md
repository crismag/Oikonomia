**People** is the church directory. It is under **Organization** in the Leadership part of the sidebar.

There is one record for each person, and the rest of Oikonomia refers to it. The same person is the same record on a ministry team, an attendance list and a Reach-Out report — nothing keeps its own copy.

### Finding someone

- Type in **Search people by name or role**.
- Choose a campus to narrow the list, or **All campuses** to see everyone.

Each row shows the person's name, what they are called here and their campus, and on wider screens the ministries they serve in. Choose a row to open [their page](topic:people.person-page).

The search and campus filter are kept in the page address, so a narrowed list can be bookmarked or shared.

### What a role name means

"What they are called here" — Pastor, LifeGroup leader, Bishop — is a description the church uses. It grants nothing. What someone may do comes from [confirmed assignments](topic:assignments.claims) and the church's own roles.

### Changing a record

People are added and corrected by an administrator in Administration, not from this page.
