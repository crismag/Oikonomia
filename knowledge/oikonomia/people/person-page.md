A person's page gathers what the binder already holds about them. Every section is filtered to what **you** may see. Opening someone's page never widens your access.

### Beside the page

- **Ministries** — the ministries they are on.
- **Responsibilities** — every assignment, with its state: confirmed, _awaiting confirmation_, _correction requested_, or _ended_. This is read-only; confirming happens in Administration.
- **Reporting** — who they report to, and the people who report to them.
- **Campus**.

### In the page

- **Work and reports** — work records involving them that you may read. If some related records are closed to you, the page says how many and does not list them.
- **Leadership reports** they wrote that you may already read.
- **Reach-Out** reports they wrote or worked on.
- **Meeting notes** they wrote that you may read. Someone else's personal notes never appear here.
- **Gatherings they lead** and **Gatherings attended**.
- **Activity** — their recent activity on work you may see.

Apart from Work and reports and Activity, a section with nothing to show is left out. Lists show up to six of the newest items; the module itself has the rest.

> A report whose audience does not include you is not listed, and nothing hints that it exists.
