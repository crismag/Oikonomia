When a record's audience does not include you, Oikonomia does not say "you may not see this". It behaves as if the record did not exist: it is not listed, search does not find it, and opening a report's address directly shows **Page not found**.

That is deliberate. Telling you a report exists but is closed would already disclose that somebody wrote about someone.

### Likely reasons

- **The audience does not include you.** A Leadership Report reaches the readers its author chose.
- **It is somebody's personal note.** Personal meeting notes are their author's alone.
- **Your assignment is not confirmed yet.** Access that follows from where you serve starts only once an administrator confirms it. See [Assignment still pending](topic:troubleshooting.assignment-pending).
- **It was deleted.**

Rank does not change this. Administrators and senior leaders get the same answer for records they are not an audience for.

### What to do

Ask the person who wrote it, or who sent you the link, whether you should be able to read it. Only they can widen its audience. An administrator can check whether your assignments are confirmed, but administering Oikonomia does not let them open the record for you.
