A meeting note is the record of what was said. When part of it needs to reach people who were not in the room as a report, start a Leadership Report from the note.

### Steps

1. Open the note from [Meeting Notes](destination:meeting-notes). This works on a note you are writing and on minutes you are only reading.
2. Choose **Write a report from this meeting**, at the top beside **Print**.
3. A new report opens for editing. It is a **Draft**, you are its author, and its title is the meeting's title.
4. Write it, choose who can read it, and move it on when it is ready. See [Write a leadership report](topic:reports.create).

### Worth knowing

- Nothing is copied from the note into the report. Write what the report needs to say.
- A new report is private to you until you choose its audience. Drafts are only ever yours.
- The report shows **Meeting** at the top, which opens the note it came from. That link grants nothing: someone who may read the report cannot open the note unless they may already read it.
- Reports written from the note that you may read are listed under **Reports from this meeting** at the bottom of the note.
