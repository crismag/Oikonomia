The kind of note is chosen when you create it, and it decides who may read it. There is no separate sharing setting; the sentence beside the tags states the rule for that note.

### Personal note

Your own working record. It need not cover everything that happened.

- **Only you can read it.** Participants, administrators and senior leaders cannot.
- For anyone else it does not appear in their list, and a link to it says it was not found. Even that it exists stays private.

### Meeting minutes

The meeting's record, for the people it concerned.

- **The people added as participants can read it**, as well as the person who wrote it.
- Only the writer can change it. A participant reads it but cannot edit it.

### Tasks from a note

A task keeps its own rules. Whoever a task is given to sees it on their week and can tick it done, even if they may not read the note. Then it is labelled "From a meeting" and the note is not linked.

Meeting Notes offers no way to share a personal note with someone, or to change a note's kind once it is created.
