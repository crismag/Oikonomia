A task from a meeting is its own record. Changing or deleting the line afterwards does not change or remove the task.

### Create one

1. In a note you wrote, click into a line that has text.
2. Choose **Create task** in the toolbar. It is unavailable on an empty line.
3. The task appears under **Meeting activity**, below the note. It starts **assigned to you**, and the due date is selected so you can fill it in next.

### Reaching a week

A task reaches a week only when it has **both** someone assigned and a due date. Nothing picks a date for you. The line under each task says where it stands:

- _Assign someone and add a due date to put it on a week._
- _Add a due date to put it on a week._
- **On your week** with the date — a link to that day on Weekly Agenda.
- **On** (name)**'s week** with the date.

Change the person with the assignee list; clear the date and it leaves the week again.

### Completing it

Tick the box beside the task in the note, or on the week. Only the person it was given to and the note's writer may change it. A done task stays listed under the meeting, ticked.
