Meeting Notes keeps what was said and decided, written down while it is fresh. It is under My Work in the sidebar.

### The list

Only notes you may read are listed, newest first. Each row shows the date, the title, whether it is a personal note or minutes, the ministry it relates to, and counts of open tasks, decisions and unresolved follow-ups. **Draft** shows until the note is marked complete. See [Find a past meeting](topic:meeting-notes.find).

### A note

- **New meeting note** asks one question: **Personal note** or **Meeting minutes**. That choice decides who can read it — see [Personal notes and meeting minutes](topic:meeting-notes.personal-vs-minutes).
- Write in lines; the toolbar changes a line into a heading, list, checklist, decision or follow-up. See [Write a meeting note](topic:meeting-notes.write).
- **Create task** turns a line into a task that can reach someone's week. See [Tasks from a meeting](topic:meeting-notes.tasks).
- **Mark complete**, **Print**, and *Does this need anything from leadership?* sit on the note. See [Finish, print or ask leadership](topic:meeting-notes.complete-and-print).

Writing saves as you go; the indicator beside **Print** says **Saving…**, **Saved** or **Up to date**, or shows what went wrong with **Try again**.

There is no way to delete a meeting note from this page.
