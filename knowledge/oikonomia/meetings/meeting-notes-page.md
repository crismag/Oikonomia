Meeting Notes keeps what was said and decided, written down while it is fresh.

### The list

Only notes you may read are listed. Search meetings and tags, narrow to **Personal** or **Minutes**, pick a ministry, or choose a tag. Each row shows open tasks, decisions and unresolved follow-ups, and **Draft** until the note is marked complete.

**New meeting note** asks one question: **Personal note** or **Meeting minutes**. Minutes open with Attendees, Agenda, Discussion, Decisions, Action items and Follow-up; a personal note opens on an empty line. You can rename, delete or add sections either way.

### Writing

- Set the title, date, time, meeting type and participants. **Related to** links a ministry; tags describe the note.
- The toolbar has bold, italic, underline, links, headings, lists, checklists, quotes and dividers.
- **Mark as decision** and **Follow-up** mark a line. A follow-up can be marked resolved and reopened.
- **Create task** turns the selected line into a task — see [Meeting tasks](topic:meeting-notes.tasks).

Writing saves as you go; the indicator says **Saving…**, **Saved**, or what went wrong. If the previous meeting of the same type left items unresolved, **Bring into this meeting** copies them in. **Mark complete** ends the draft, **Print** gives a paper copy, and _Does this need anything from leadership?_ asks another leader for something.

There is no way to delete a meeting note from this page.
