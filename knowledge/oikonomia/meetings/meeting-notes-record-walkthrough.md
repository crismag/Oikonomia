Six steps, from the start of a meeting to what it agreed being on the weeks of the people carrying it.

## Start the minutes

Destination: meeting-notes

Open Meeting Notes, choose **New meeting note**, then **Meeting minutes**. For notes only you should read, choose **Personal note** instead — the rest of the steps are the same.

## Say what meeting it was

Give it a title, check the date and time, and choose the meeting type. Choose **+ Add participants** and pick who was there, then **Done** — for minutes, they are who can read the note. Set **Related to** if it was a ministry's meeting.

## Write as you go

Write under the headings the minutes start with. Press Enter for a new line. If the previous meeting of this type left things open, choose **Bring into this meeting** to add them.

## Mark decisions and follow-ups

Click into a line that records what was agreed and choose **Mark as decision**. For something still open, choose **Follow-up**; later choose **Mark resolved** beside it.

## Turn actions into tasks

Click into a line that someone agreed to do and choose **Create task**. Under **Meeting activity**, choose who carries it and give it a due date. The line under the task says **On your week** or **On** (name)**'s week** once it has both.

## Mark it complete

Choose **Mark complete** at the top. Choose **Print** if you want a paper copy. If something needs leadership outside the room, use *Does this need anything from leadership?* at the bottom of the note.
