### Start the note

1. Open Meeting Notes and choose **New meeting note**.
2. Choose **Personal note** (your own working record) or **Meeting minutes** (the meeting's record for the people it concerned). You cannot change this later.

Minutes open with the headings Attendees, Agenda, Discussion, Decisions, Action items and Follow-up and next meeting. A personal note opens on one empty line. You can rewrite or remove any of it.

### Meeting details

At the top of the note:

- the **title** ("What meeting is this?");
- the **date** and **time**;
- the **meeting type** — Leadership, Ministry, Planning, Committee, One to one or Other. Setting it lets the next meeting of the same type pick up what this one left open;
- **+ Add participants** — choose people, then **Done**. For minutes, participants are who can read the note;
- **Related to** — a ministry (or your own words). A ministry's page lists the meetings related to it;
- **+ tag** — type a tag and press Enter. Existing tags are suggested as you type.

The sentence at the end of that row says who can read this note.

### Writing

Each line is its own block. **Enter** starts the next line; **Backspace** on an empty line removes it. The toolbar acts on the line you are in:

- **Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline**, **Link** and **Clear formatting** act on selected text.
- **Heading 1**, **Heading 2**, **Bullet list**, **Numbered list**, **Checklist**, **Quote** and **Divider** change the line.
- **Mark as decision** and **Follow-up** mark what was agreed and what is still open — see [Decisions and follow-ups](topic:meeting-notes.decisions-follow-ups).
- **Create task** — see [Tasks from a meeting](topic:meeting-notes.tasks).
- **Delete block** (the bin at the right) removes the line.

Everything saves as you type. Only the person who wrote the note can change it.
