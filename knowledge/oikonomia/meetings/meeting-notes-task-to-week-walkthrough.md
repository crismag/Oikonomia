Five steps, from something agreed in a meeting to a task on the week of the person carrying it. You need a note you wrote.

## Open the note

Destination: meeting-notes

Open Meeting Notes and choose the meeting. If it is not written yet, choose **New meeting note** and write the line that says what needs doing.

## Create the task

Click into that line, then choose **Create task** in the toolbar. The task appears under **Meeting activity** below the note, assigned to you.

## Choose who carries it

In the task's assignee list, keep yourself or choose the leader who agreed to do it. Leave it as **Unassigned** and it reaches nobody's week.

## Give it a due date

Pick the date in the task's date field. The line under the task changes to **On your week** or **On** (name)**'s week**, with the day. Without a date, the task stays in the meeting and nothing is put on a week.

## Check the week

Destination: weekly-agenda

The task is on the assignee's Weekly Agenda on its due date, under **To do**. They can tick it done there; opening it from the List or Calendar view comes back to the meeting note if they may read it.
