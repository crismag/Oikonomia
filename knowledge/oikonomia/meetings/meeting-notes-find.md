Open Meeting Notes. Above the list:

- **Search meetings and tags** finds notes whose title, text or tags contain what you type.
- **All**, **Personal** and **Minutes** narrow to one kind of note. Choose the same one again to clear it.
- **Any context** is a list of the ministries your notes relate to. Pick one to see only its meetings.
- The **#tag** buttons below narrow to one tag. Choose it again to clear it.

These combine. When nothing fits, the list says **No meeting matches**; clear a filter or change the search. Long lists are split into pages at the bottom. Opening a note and choosing **Meeting Notes** at the top brings you back to the same filtered list.

### Other places meetings appear

- A ministry's page lists the meeting notes **Related to** that ministry.
- A person's page lists meeting notes they wrote or took down, when you may read them.

Every list shows only notes you may read. A personal note written by someone else never appears, for anyone — see [Why can't someone see my meeting note?](topic:meeting-notes.cant-see).
