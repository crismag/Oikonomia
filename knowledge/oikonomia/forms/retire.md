A filled-in record is a statement of what was done, so deleting a form never deletes its records.

Only the form's owner sees **Delete form** on the form's page. What happens next depends on whether anyone has used it.

### Nothing filled in

The form is deleted. Oikonomia asks first — **Delete the form** — because there is no undo.

### Records exist

The button reads **Retire the form**. The form is retired rather than deleted:

- Every record made with it is kept, and still opens as it was filled in.
- No new records can be started from it; **Use** is no longer offered.
- It moves to **Retired forms** in the form library, with its record count, and its page says when it was retired.

There is no way to delete a form that has records, and there is no control to bring a retired form back. To keep using a design, **Copy** it from the form library _before_ you delete it — the copy starts its own life with no records.
