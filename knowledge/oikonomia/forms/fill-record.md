### Make a record

1. Open [Forms](destination:forms) (from Documents & Forms, **Open form library**).
2. On the form, choose **Use**. The form itself is not changed.
3. Give it a **Record name**, such as the week it covers, and optionally a **Period**.
4. Choose **Create record**. The record opens.

### Fill it in

Answer each item on the sheet. Every answer is saved as you give it.

- A **status item** can be set to Not started, In progress, Done, Needs attention or N/A.
- Where an item offers **Add a note**, write anything worth recording beside it.

**Standing**, beside the sheet, counts what is done, what needs attention and what is N/A. **History** lists the latest changes and who made them.

### Finish it

Choose **Mark completed**. The record can no longer be changed. To correct it, choose **Reopen record**.

### Print it

Choose **Print this record**, then **Print this record** again on the print view. It prints the record as it currently stands. **Back to the record** returns you.

### If the form has changed

A record keeps the version it was made from. If the form was updated since, the record says so, and nothing in your record changes.

Back at the top, **Forms** returns to the form library.
