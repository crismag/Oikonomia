Open **Forms** from Documents & Forms (**Open form library**). Two things are kept apart here: the **form** — a reusable design — and the **records** made by filling it in.

### The form library

Search by title, section or field label, or narrow by ministry. Each form offers:

- **Use** — makes a record to fill in. The form is unchanged.
- **Edit** — opens the design.
- **Copy** — makes another design you can change separately, for a different ministry or a new year.

**Create form** starts from a template, which you can rename and reshape. **Recent records** and any **Retired forms** are listed below.

### Designing a form

Click a field to change its label, guidance and options in the side panel. Add, move, duplicate and delete sections and fields. **Preview** shows what someone filling it in will see.

Nothing is kept until you choose **Save as v2** (the next version number). Only the form's owner can change its design; anyone who can open the ministry can use it.

### Records

A record keeps the version it was filled under. Saving a new design later never changes an existing record, and the record says so if the form has moved on.

Choose **Mark completed** when it is done; a completed record cannot be changed until you **Reopen record**.

### Printing

**Print blank** on a form prints the empty sheet. **Print this record** prints a record as it currently stands.
