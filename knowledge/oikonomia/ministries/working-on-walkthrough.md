Four steps, from the list of ministries to what one of them is doing this year.

## Open your ministry

Destination: ministries

**Ministry** is under **Shared** in the sidebar. The ministries you lead or serve in are the cards at the top; choose one.

## Read the Overview

Announcements sit at the top. **Recent documents** shows what was filed last, the goals box counts this year's goals, and **Meeting notes** lists the ministry's latest meetings — choose one to open it.

## Check its goals

Choose the **Goals** view. The ministry's own goals come first, each with its status; choose one to see its progress. Below, **Leaders' own goals that relate to this ministry** are grouped under each leader's name — they are personal goals, not the ministry's.

## See what changed recently

Choose **Activity** for progress noted on the ministry's goals and meetings recorded for it, newest first. For anything filed, use **Documents**.
