A ministry's goals are set in Goals and shown on the ministry's page. You need to lead or serve in the ministry.

### Steps

1. Open the ministry and choose the **Goals** view.
2. Choose **All ministry goals**. Goals opens on **Ministry goals** for this year.
3. Choose **Add goal**. **A ministry's** is already selected under *Whose goal is it?*
4. Choose the ministry. Only ministries you lead or serve in are offered.
5. Write the goal, pick a **Target month — optional**, and choose **Add**.

The goal now appears on the ministry's **Goals** view and counts on its **Overview**.

### Worth knowing

- **New → Goals** on the ministry also opens Goals, starting on your personal goals. Switch *Whose goal is it?* to **A ministry's**.
- A goal set as **Mine** with *Relates to a ministry* stays your personal goal. It is listed on the ministry under **Leaders' own goals that relate to this ministry**, under your name, and is not counted as the ministry's progress.
- If Add goal says *You do not lead or serve in a ministry*, your assignment is not confirmed yet. See [Your relationship to a ministry](topic:ministries.relationship).
