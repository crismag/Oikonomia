### Steps

1. Open **Ministry** and choose the ministry.
2. On **Overview**, **Recent documents** shows the latest few. Choose **All documents**, or the **Documents** view, for everything.
3. Type in **Search documents** to match titles and descriptions, or choose a kind — the chips show only kinds this ministry actually has.
4. Choose a document. Plans, reports, announcements and checklists written in Oikonomia open here for writing. A link or Drive document opens its page in the binder — where it lives, where it is filed, who registered it — with **Open** to go to the document itself.
5. To go straight to a link or Drive document instead, choose **Open** (or **Open in Drive**) at the end of its row. It opens in a new tab.

Where Google Drive is connected, a Drive document also shows who owns it and when it last changed.

### Worth knowing

- The same documents are found in [Resource Search](destination:resource-search).
- Oikonomia records where a document lives. Whether a Drive or web document opens for you is decided by its own sharing there.
- Filed under the wrong ministry, or named wrongly? See [Edit or unfile a document](topic:documents.edit-unfile).
- To file a document under the ministry, see [Documents in Google Drive](topic:documents.drive), [Add a link to a ministry](topic:ministries.add-link) or [register it from Documents & Forms](topic:documents.register).
