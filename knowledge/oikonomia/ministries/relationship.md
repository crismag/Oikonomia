Where a ministry appears on the list, and what its page offers you, depends on how you stand in relation to it.

- **Lead** — *You lead this ministry*. Everything a team member can do, plus **Add existing material** (Add link, Add from Drive) in the **New** menu.
- **Serve** (on the team) — *You serve here*. **New** is offered: set the ministry's goals and change them, start plans, reports, announcements and checklists, and write meeting notes for it.
- **Shared with you** — listed under **Shared with you**. You can open the ministry and read what is shared, but it is not membership: no **New**, and you cannot set or change its goals.
- **None** — listed under **Other ministries**. You can open it and see what it keeps that you may read.

### Joining a ministry

Leading and serving come from confirmed assignments. Say where you serve, and an administrator confirms it; until then nothing changes on the ministry's page. See [Saying where you serve](topic:assignments.claims).
