Oikonomia records **where a document lives**. It does not store the file.

Where your church has connected Google Drive, **Add from Drive** opens Drive itself instead — see [Documents in Google Drive](topic:documents.drive). The steps below are for a link, and for Drive where it is not connected.

### Steps

1. Open **Ministry** and choose the ministry you lead.
2. Choose **New**. Under **Add existing material**, choose **Add link** or **Add from Drive**.
3. For Drive where it is not connected, copy the document's link in Google Drive first; the form says Drive is not connected on this installation.
4. Fill in **What is it called?** and **Where is it?** (the address). Both are required.
5. Pick **What kind of thing is it?** and, if useful, add a note. The ministry is already filled in.
6. Choose **Register document**.

It now appears on the ministry's **Documents** view and in Resource Search. Opening it goes to the address you gave.

### Worth knowing

- **Add link** is shown only to the ministry's lead. Team members can still register a document from [Documents & Forms](topic:documents.register) and choose this ministry, and, where Drive is connected, use **Add from Drive**.
- Nothing is copied. The document's own sharing — in Drive or wherever it lives — still decides who can open it.
- Oikonomia never stores files. Uploading is possible only where Drive is connected, and the file goes into Drive.
