**Ministry** is under **Shared** in the sidebar. A ministry exists independently of whoever leads it, so its work stays with it when the lead changes.

### The list

Search by name or purpose, or narrow by campus. Ministries you lead or serve in come first, as cards showing what is filed and how many goals there are. Below are **Shared with you** and **Other ministries**.

### A ministry's page

The header names the campus, who leads it and how many are on the team. Four views:

- **Overview** — any announcements, recent documents, this year's goals and the ministry's meeting notes.
- **Goals** — the ministry's own goals for this year, then **Leaders' own goals that relate to this ministry**, grouped under each leader's name. Those stay that leader's personal goals; they are not counted as the ministry's progress.
- **Documents** — everything filed under the ministry, searchable and filterable by kind.
- **Activity** — progress noted on its goals and notes from its meetings.

### The New menu

Only people who lead or serve in the ministry see **New**. Under **Create in the binder**, it offers Goals, Meeting Note and Form, and starts a Plan, Report, Announcement or Checklist the ministry keeps.

Only the lead also sees **Add existing material**: [Add link and Add from Drive](topic:ministries.add-link). **Upload file** is marked Soon — Oikonomia does not store files.

Being shared with a ministry lets you read what is shared; it is not membership, so you are not offered New.
