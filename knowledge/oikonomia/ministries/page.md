**Ministry** is under **Shared** in the sidebar. A ministry keeps its own work, so its documents and goals stay with it when the lead changes.

### The list

**Search ministries** by name or purpose, or narrow to one campus. Ministries you lead or serve in come first, as cards showing the campus, *You lead this ministry* or *You serve here*, and what is filed — documents and goals. Below them are **Shared with you** and **Other ministries**. Choose any ministry to open it.

### A ministry's page

The header names the campus, who leads it and how many are on the team, and — if you serve in it or it is shared with you — your relationship. Four views:

- **Overview** — any announcements, **Recent documents**, this year's goals (active and completed), and the ministry's **Meeting notes**.
- **Goals** — the ministry's own goals for this year, then **Leaders' own goals that relate to this ministry**. See [Add a ministry goal](topic:ministries.add-goal).
- **Documents** — everything filed under the ministry. See [Find a ministry's documents](topic:ministries.find-documents).
- **Activity** — progress noted on its goals and notes recorded at its meetings, newest first.

### The New menu

People who lead or serve in the ministry see **New**. Under **Create in the binder**: **Goals**, **Meeting Note** and **Form** open those pages, and **Plan**, **Report**, **Announcement** and **Checklist** start a document the ministry keeps and open it for writing.

Only the lead also sees **Add existing material**: [Add link and Add from Drive](topic:ministries.add-link). **Upload file** is marked Soon — Oikonomia does not store files.

What you can do depends on your place in the ministry. See [Your relationship to a ministry](topic:ministries.relationship).
