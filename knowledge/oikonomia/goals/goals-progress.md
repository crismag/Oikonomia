Open the goal from [Goals](destination:goals). These actions appear only for whoever may change the goal — see [Whose a goal is](topic:goals.scope).

### Add a progress note

Under **Progress**, type in "Add a progress note" and choose **Add**. Notes are listed newest first, with who wrote them.

### Change where it stands

The **Actions** panel beside the goal offers what fits its status:

- **Mark complete** (active goals) — with an optional completion note. The goal shows as Completed.
- **Put on hold** (active goals) — with an optional reason, shown on the goal.
- **Resume goal** (goals on hold) — makes it active again.
- **Carry to** (next year) — creates the same goal in the next year, pointing back to this one, and marks this one **Carried forward**. The old year keeps its record. Offered for any goal not already carried forward.

Each of these is also added to the goal's progress, so the history says what happened and when.

If someone else changed the goal after your page loaded — completed it, put it on hold — your action is refused with **This goal was changed somewhere else while you were working.** The page then shows the goal as it now stands, so you can decide again.

### Asking for help

Progress notes ask nothing of anybody. To ask a leader for guidance, a decision, or help with something blocking the goal, use _Does this need anything from leadership?_ on the goal's page.

### Not available

The goal's page has no way to rename a goal, change its description or target, or delete it.
