Every goal starts with whose it is, chosen when it is set. That choice decides who may change it.

- **Mine** — a personal goal: your own goal as a leader. Only you may change it.
- **A ministry's** — what the ministry wants to improve. Anyone who leads or serves in that ministry may change it.
- **A group's** — a goal of a group the church has named, such as a council or a team. That group's members may change it.

"Change" means adding progress notes, completing, holding, resuming and carrying forward. Leaders who may not change a goal can still open it and read its progress, unless it is held to a narrower audience.

### Relates to a ministry

A personal goal may say which ministry it relates to. It stays yours — only you can change it — and it is listed on that ministry's page under your name, after the ministry's own goals.

### Where you serve

Setting or changing a ministry's goal needs you to lead or serve in that ministry; a ministry merely shared with you does not count. A group's goal needs you to be a member. When that is not the case, **Add goal** says there is no ministry or group to set a goal for. A claim about where you serve that is still waiting for an administrator's confirmation grants nothing.
