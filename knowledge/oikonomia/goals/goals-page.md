Goals keeps the year's goals apart by whose they are. Pick the year at the top; **Add goal** sets a new one.

### The tabs

- **Personal goals** — your own. Other leaders' personal goals are not listed here; you reach them from Reports to you or that person's page.
- **Ministry goals** — each ministry's goals under its name. Ministries you work in are open and marked **Yours**; the rest stay closed until you open them.
- **Other groups** — goals of groups the church has named, such as a council or a team, grouped the same way.

### Needs attention

Drawn only from the tab you are looking at. It lists active goals whose target month is within 30 days or already past, soonest first. A goal on hold or completed is never late.

### Finding a goal

Search titles and descriptions, or narrow by status: **Active**, **Completed**, **On hold**, **Carried forward**. Star a goal to use the **Starred** filter. If some goals are held to a narrower audience, the page says how many were not listed.

### A goal's page

The goal's page shows whose it is, its target, its progress notes, and — for whoever may change it — **Actions**. See [Record progress on a goal](topic:goals.progress). Progress is a record of notes and status changes, never a percentage.
