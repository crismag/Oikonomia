Five steps, from an empty year to a goal you can record progress against.

## Open Goals

Destination: goals

Goals is under My Work in the sidebar. Choose the year at the top of the page — the goal is set for the year you are looking at.

## Choose Add goal

Choose **Add goal**. It starts on the kind of goal for the tab you are on.

## Say whose goal it is

Under _Whose goal is it?_ choose **Mine**, **A ministry's** or **A group's**.

- For a ministry's goal, choose the **Ministry**. Only ministries you lead or serve in are offered.
- For a group's goal, choose the **Group**. Only groups you belong to are offered.
- For your own goal, you may pick a ministry under _Relates to a ministry — optional_. It stays your goal.

## Write the goal

Type the goal in a few words — what you want to grow in, or what the ministry or group wants to improve.

## Add a target month and save

Pick a **Target month** if there is one; it is optional. Choose **Add**. The goal is numbered for that year. While it is active and its target month is within 30 days or past, it appears under **Needs attention**.
