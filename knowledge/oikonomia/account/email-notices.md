Open **Account & security** from the account menu in the top bar. Under **Email notices** there is a switch for each kind:

- **When someone asks something of you** — an ask for attention, action or approval made of you by name, or of a position you hold for the person asking (for example, their reporting leader).
- **When someone gives you a meeting task** — a task in a meeting note somebody else keeps, assigned to you.

Both are off until you turn them on. Turn one off the same way.

### What the email says

Who asked or assigned it, what it is, the date if there is one, and a link to open it in Oikonomia. It never includes the content of a report or a note. A task from a meeting note you may not read does not name the meeting; its link opens your week instead.

You are never emailed about something you did yourself, and nothing else is emailed: reports arriving and past-due work stay in the bell and on Home.

### When no email arrives

- The section says **This installation cannot send email** when your church has not set up mail. Your choice is kept for when it does.
- It says your record has **no email address** when there is nowhere to send it. Your church administrator can add one.

> In the public demo, email is switched off, so nothing is sent.
