Open **Account & security** from the account menu in the top bar.

### Sign-in methods

Lists **Password**, **Google** and **Email sign-in link**, with when each was set or whether it is available on this installation.

### Sign out a device

**Where you are signed in** lists every signed-in device, when it signed in and when it was last used.

1. Find the device you do not recognise.
2. Choose **Sign out this device**.

To end everything except where you are now, choose **Sign out of all other devices**. The device you are using is not offered — use **Sign out** in the account menu for that.

### Changing your password

This page has no password form. To choose a new password, sign out and use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen; it appears only where the installation can send email. Otherwise ask your church administrator to set one for you.

Setting a new password signs out every session, so anyone who knew the old one loses access immediately.

> In the public demo, signing other devices out is switched off.
