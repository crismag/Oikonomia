Open the account menu in the top right and choose **Appearance**. It opens **Account & security** at the **Appearance** section.

### Theme

Pick one of four. Each has its own colours and typefaces:

- **Stained glass** — jewel colours on a bright page, with a deep indigo sidebar.
- **Vineyard** — olive, wheat and plum; warm and earthy.
- **Daybreak** — bright and friendly, with soft tints.
- **Quiet** — the original: slate, one teal accent, little colour.

The change shows straight away.

### Light or dark

Choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **Match this device** to follow your phone or computer's own setting as it changes.

### Worth knowing

- The choice is kept on **this browser** only. Another device, or another person, chooses their own.
- Each area of work has its own colour in every theme — the week, meetings, Reach-Out, reports, LifeGroup and so on — in the sidebar, the board at the top of each page, and Home. The colour helps you find your place; the words always say the same thing.
