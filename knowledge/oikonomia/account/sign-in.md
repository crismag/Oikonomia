Oikonomia is by invitation. There is no public sign-up: an administrator adds you as a person and invites you. The invitation email carries a link that works once and expires in fifteen minutes; following it asks you to choose a password of at least 12 characters.

### Sign in

1. Enter your **Email or username** and **Password**.
2. Choose **Sign in**.

If either half is wrong the screen says only _Those details were not recognised._ After several failed attempts you are asked to wait a few minutes before trying again.

### Other ways in

- **Continue with Google** appears only where your church has connected Google sign-in.
- **Email me a sign-in link** and **Forgot password?** appear only where the installation can send email. A link is good for fifteen minutes.

### When email is not set up

The sign-in screen says that sign-in links and password resets are unavailable. Your church administrator can set a new password for you — there is no way to reset it yourself.

> Signing in proves who you are. What you can see comes from assignments your church confirms.
