Asks you made are at the bottom of the [Leadership Inbox](destination:inbox).

### What you are waiting on

Each open ask shows what you asked, who it was **Asked of**, where it stands, and anything said on it since. **Open context** opens the record it came from.

- **Answer** — shown when the leader chose **Ask a question**; the row says _Waiting on your answer_ and shows their question. Write your answer and choose **Send answer**. It goes back to them and the ask is _Requested_ again.
- **Withdraw** — takes back an ask that is still open. Confirm with **Withdraw the ask**. It is removed for the people it was sent to, with anything said on it, and cannot be undone.

You cannot approve, complete or note your own ask; those belong to the leader you asked.

### Answered recently

Asks you made that were settled in the last two weeks — noted, completed, could not be done, approved or declined — with any reason the leader gave. An answered ask cannot be withdrawn: the outcome stays on the record.

> What is said on an ask is shown only to you and the leader you asked. Other readers of the record see the ask and its status, not the notes.
