When another leader asks you for an **action**, you can give the work a day on your week. **Put on my week** is offered in two places:

- on the ask's row in the [Leadership Inbox](destination:inbox);
- in the **Asked of you** card on the record the ask came from — a leadership report, a Reach-Out report, a meeting note or a work record.

### What it does

It adds an item to your [Weekly Agenda](destination:weekly-agenda) with the words of the request. The day is the ask's **needed-by** date while that is still ahead; otherwise today. If the ask was still _Requested_, it moves to _In progress_.

The button then reads **On your week**, which opens that day. If you complete the agenda item, the button is offered again.

### Take it on, or Put on my week

- **Take it on** (inbox only) changes the ask's status to _In progress_. Nothing goes on your week.
- **Put on my week** does both: a dated item on your week, and _In progress_.

Neither finishes the ask. When the work is done, choose **Completed** in the Leadership Inbox — or **Can't do it**, with a note.

Only actions offer this. Attention is noted; an approval is decided.
