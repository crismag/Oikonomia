Five steps, from an action somebody asked of you to work that has a day and gets finished.

## Open the Leadership Inbox

Destination: inbox

Leadership Inbox is under Oversight in the sidebar. Choose **Actions** to see only what somebody asked you to do.

## Read the ask where it came from

Choose **Open context** on the row to open the report, note or record it came from. If you are on a leadership report or Reach-Out report, the **Asked of you** card there offers the same next step.

## Put it on your week

Choose **Put on my week**. It lands on the needed-by date if that is still ahead, otherwise today, and the ask moves to _In progress_. The button becomes **On your week**.

## Do the work

Destination: weekly-agenda

Choose **On your week**, or open the Weekly Agenda. The item carries the words of the request. Tick it off when you are done.

## Close the ask

Destination: inbox

Back in the Leadership Inbox, choose **Completed** on the ask — or **Can't do it** and say why. Ticking the agenda item does not close the ask; the person who asked sees it settled only when you do this.
