# Shared work

Two surfaces where a team works together rather than one person keeping a
record.

## LifeGroup

> _The shared schedule. Add the week's gatherings, claim what you can lead, and
> fill in the details together._

The schedule is a list of rows, one per gathering the week expects. The
important property is stated on the empty page:

> _A row can sit here needing a leader until someone picks it up._

That is the whole design. The week's shape is agreed first; who leads each
gathering is settled separately, by people claiming what they can take. A
gathering without a leader is a normal, visible state — not an error and not a
blank to be hidden.

### A gathering

Date, time, venue and who is leading. The **venue is reused and never owned**
by a gathering — the same home or room appears across weeks, and renaming it
once renames it everywhere.

### Attendance

Marked at the gathering. Participation is recorded against the canonical
person records, not a separate list kept by the group, so the same person is
the same person across ministries, outreach and attendance.

### Entries and the gathering report

Entries are written collaboratively during or after a gathering, each with its
own visibility. After it, a gathering report records what happened.

> A gathering with attendance taken and no report written shows as **in
> progress**, not done. Saying otherwise would tell a leader they had finished
> something they had not.

## Ministry

> _Where each ministry keeps its own work._

Searchable, and filterable by campus.

A ministry exists **independently of whoever currently leads it**, which is why
one can be created before a lead is chosen and why a lead changing does not
disturb its work.

Each ministry keeps its own documents, goals and reports. Who may write in them
is the ministry's own question — its lead and the people on its team.

A ministry's lead can add material that lives elsewhere from **New → Add link**
or **Add from Drive**. Both register the document's address and file it under
the ministry; nothing is copied, and Oikonomia does not connect to Drive — the
document's own sharing still decides who can open it. **Upload file** is marked
Soon: the binder does not store files.

> **Being shared with a ministry is not membership.** A leader who can read a
> ministry's document may not have a cursor in it, and is told so plainly
> rather than shown controls that refuse when pressed.

## Where you serve

Membership in a ministry or a responsibility group is an **assignment**, and
only a confirmed one counts.

You can say where you serve. That is recorded as a claim and appears in
Administration under _Awaiting confirmation_. Until an administrator confirms
it, it grants nothing — no membership, no readership, no export scope — and you
cannot confirm your own.

If a record about where you serve is wrong, you can ask for a correction, which
appears in the same place for an administrator to look at.

An assignment that has ended is kept as history rather than deleted, because
who led something last year is part of the record.
